## Notes

- Alterac Pass: the "Mercenary Camps" section does not contain the standard "Mercenaries are
  disabled/enabled during the Primary Objectives" sentence found on other pages. Instead it
  says camps disappear once Cavalry is activated and reappear once all Cavalry units are
  killed, which was read as equivalent to "disabled during the objective."
- Hanamura Temple: the Recon Camp is described under "Areas of interest," not the "Mercenary
  Camps" section, and its entry has no Spawns/respawn line, so first spawn and respawn are
  unstated.
- Haunted Mines: the page has no standard "Mercenary Camps" section with Amount/Spawns/Location
  fields — camps are only briefly named and located under "Areas of interest," with no spawn or
  respawn timers and no statement on whether camps are disabled during the objective. The camp
  type (siege/bruiser/boss/special) is also not stated for the Sapper camp; "Siege Giant" is
  inferred as siege type only because "siege" appears in its own description text.
- Volskaya Foundry: the sentence describing whether Mercenaries are disabled during the
  objective is grammatically broken ("Mercenaries while the Triglav Protector is active."),
  missing what appears to be the word "disabled." Read as "Mercenaries are disabled while the
  Triglav Protector is active."
